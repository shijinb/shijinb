### About me

I lead engineering for AI-powered SaaS products, from architecture and delivery to production operations. Over 12 years I've moved from building PHP web applications to leading full-stack teams that ship AI recruitment products, multi-module business platforms and the cloud infrastructure they run on.

- **AI products:** recruitment AI that discovers, engages, evaluates and ranks candidates, including joining-probability scoring, built on OpenAI, Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude.
- **SaaS platforms:** CRM, HRM, ATS, asset tracking, bulk mailer, and helpdesk & ticketing systems.
- **Engineering leadership:** technical delivery across frontend, backend, APIs, databases, integrations and infrastructure; code review, planning and mentoring.
- **Cloud & reliability:** AWS, Linux, Nginx, SSL/TLS, deployments, background workers and production troubleshooting.
- **Search & AI discoverability:** technical SEO, structured data, crawlability and indexing, plus AEO/GEO for AI-powered search.
